# 🍽️ **WHAT YOU’RE BUILDING**

You are building a **full recipe–sharing platform** where:

### ✔ Users can:

* Browse recipes
* View recipe details (ingredients, steps, time, difficulty)
* Search for recipes (by ingredient, name, type)
* Filter by category (Breakfast, Nigerian dishes, Snacks, Drinks, etc.)
* Create an account
* Upload their own recipes
* Comment on recipes
* Like/save recipes

Think of it like **a simple version of AllRecipes + social media features**.

---

# 🧱 **HOW IT SHOULD LOOK — PAGE BY PAGE**

## ⭐ 1. **Home Page**

Clean hero section + categories.

### Sections:

* **Navbar**: Home | Recipes | Categories | Login/Signup
* **Hero Banner**: “Discover & Share Your Favorite Recipes”
* **Search Bar**: Big at the center
* **Popular Categories**: (Nigerian Food, Breakfast, Rice, Drinks…)
* **Trending Recipes** (cards)
* **Call-to-action button**: “Share a Recipe”

### Sample Layout:

```
| ChefHub (logo)    Home   Recipes   Login |
----------------------------------------------------
|  Search for a recipe... [search box]     |
----------------------------------------------------
| Trending Recipes (cards grid)            |
----------------------------------------------------
| Popular Categories                        |
```

---

## ⭐ 2. **Recipe Listing Page**

A grid of recipe cards.

Each card shows:

* Image
* Title (“Jollof Rice”)
* Rating
* Time (45 mins)
* User who posted it

Also add:

* Filters (category, difficulty, cooking time)
* Sort (newest, popular)

---

## ⭐ 3. **Recipe Details Page**

This is the MOST important page.

### Should include:

* Big picture of the food
* Name of recipe
* Author (linked to profile)
* Cooking time + difficulty + servings
* Ingredients (list)
* Steps (numbered)
* Tags (e.g., Rice dish, Nigerian)
* Save / Like buttons
* Comments section

Example layout:

```
[Jollof Rice Image]
Jollof Rice
By Danny P | Rating ★★★★☆

Time: 45 mins | Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients:
- Rice
- Tomato paste
- Pepper...

Steps:
1. Parboil the rice...
2. Blend pepper...
```

---

## ⭐ 4. **Create a Recipe Page**

A form where users upload their recipe.

Inputs:

* Recipe name
* Description
* Upload image
* Cooking time
* Difficulty (Easy / Medium / Hard)
* Ingredients (maybe add + button to add multiple)
* Steps (textarea)
* Tags / category

---

## ⭐ 5. **User Profile Page**

Shows:

* User name & picture
* Bio
* Their recipes
* Saved recipes

---

## ⭐ 6. **Login/Signup**

Simple email + password authentication.

If you want to use:

* **Firebase Auth** (easiest)
* **Node.js + JWT** (custom backend)
